Set your priorities before you book

A cleaning visit is easier to plan when you can explain what you want help with. Walk through your home and note the rooms that need attention first. You might want to focus on shared spaces, bedrooms, or a few jobs that have slipped down your list. Be specific about the difference you hope to see after the visit.

A short checklist can help you keep the request clear:

  • List the rooms you want to discuss.
  • Note any particular tasks or surfaces you want addressed.
  • Mention areas you would rather leave untouched.
  • Decide how you will tell whether the visit met your expectations.

The best house cleaning arrangement is based on your priorities, not on trying to describe every detail of your home at once.

What to explain and prepare

Before the visit, gather the details you may want to share. Think about access to the home, which rooms should be included, and whether there are household instructions to follow. Put away personal items or anything you do not want handled. If a room is off limits, say so in advance.

Avoid booking with only a broad request such as “clean the house” if you have particular expectations. That can leave you and the cleaner picturing different tasks. Another common mistake is waiting until the visit to mention a special concern. Bring it up when you first discuss the job so there is time to set expectations.

If you are unsure what to prioritize, start with the rooms that affect your daily routine most. You can ask for help deciding what details to share, while keeping the final request focused on your home.
